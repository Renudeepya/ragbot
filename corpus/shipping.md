# Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United
States. Express shipping is delivered the next business day when the order is
placed before 2pm local time.

Orders ship from the warehouse nearest the delivery address. Tracking numbers
are emailed as soon as the carrier scans the package.

International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and may be subject to
customs duties, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Shipping is free on orders over 50 dollars. Below that threshold, standard
shipping costs 5 dollars and express shipping costs 15 dollars.
