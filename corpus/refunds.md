# Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method.
Once processed, your bank may take an additional 2 to 3 business days to post
the credit to your statement.

Items must be returned unused and in original packaging to qualify for a full
refund. Opened software licences and gift cards are non-refundable.

Partial refunds are issued when an order is delivered incomplete. The refund
covers the missing items plus a proportional share of the shipping cost.

If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days, contact support with your
order id and we will trace the transaction with the payment processor.
