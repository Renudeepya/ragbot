# Billing

Invoices are available under the Billing section of your account and are issued
monthly on the first day of the month.

We accept major credit cards and bank transfer for annual plans. Purchase orders
are available for enterprise customers with an annual commitment.

Plan changes take effect immediately. When upgrading mid-cycle you are charged a
prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period.

Failed payments are retried three times over 7 days before the account is
suspended. Suspended accounts retain their data for 30 days.
