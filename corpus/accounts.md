# Accounts and Sign-In

Reset your password from the sign-in page using the Forgot password link. The
reset email arrives within a few minutes and the link expires after 60 minutes.

Two-factor authentication can be enabled under Security settings. We support
authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported
because they are vulnerable to SIM-swap attacks.

Accounts are locked after 10 consecutive failed sign-in attempts and unlock
automatically after 30 minutes.

To close your account, contact support. Account closure is permanent and your
order history cannot be recovered afterwards.
